# 2.1.0.0
* Enchantments
  * Added Well Fed, a chestplate enchantment that increases the health regeneration speed from eating by 25%
  * Added Curse of Malnutrition, a curse enchantment that reduces the health regeneration speed from eating by 35%
    * Like other curses, it's not obtainable from the enchanting table or villagers, only from loot and fishing
    * Can't be on the same piece as Well Fed
  * Those only change how fast the health is regenerated, not the total amount of health regenerated by food
  * If Rune Enchanting is installed, Well Fed and Curse of Malnutrition are also available as chestplate runes
